Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Optimized Hub Media LLC ("Optimized Hub," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our website, our Database Reactivation service, and our Hub CRM (together, the "Service"). Section 4 separately describes how Optimized Hub Media LLC uses Google's Gmail API in its own internal business operations, which is distinct from the Service.

2. Information We Collect

From website visitors: basic contact information submitted through our free audit form (name, email, business type, and general lead-flow details you choose to share).

From clients: account and billing information (processed by Stripe on our behalf), and CRM access credentials or exported contact data needed to run the reactivation service.

From your CRM leads: on your behalf, we process the contact information (name, phone number, email) of dormant leads in your CRM solely to run the reactivation sequences you've authorized. In Hub CRM, you may also store lead details such as property address, loan stage, and documents that borrowers upload through a private link.

3. How We Use Information

  • To deliver the free audit and reactivation service you've requested;
  • To process payments and manage your subscription;
  • To communicate with you about your account or service delivery;
  • To improve our service and internal operations.

We do not sell your data or your leads' data to third parties.

4. Google User Data (Our Internal Business Operations)

Summary. Optimized Hub Media LLC uses Google's Gmail API in its own internal operations. It connects to one mailbox: the business account of our owner, pedro@optimizedhub.com. This is separate from our Database Reactivation and CRM services. Customers are never asked to connect a Google account.

Data we access. With the owner's authorization, our internal tools can send email from that Gmail account and can search and read messages in that mailbox. Reading is used only to find receipts, invoices, and payment notifications from software and service vendors.

How we use it.

  • To send the company's own business email: onboarding and payment confirmations to customers, audit reports a prospect requested, and internal alerts to the owner.
  • To extract the date, vendor, amount, and category from vendor receipts and add each one, as a pending entry, to the owner's private bookkeeping ledger, where the owner confirms or dismisses it.

AI processing. To extract expense details, the text of a matching receipt email is sent through our automation platform to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns the structured fields. This is done only to provide the expense-logging feature to the owner. Anthropic's commercial terms state that API inputs are not used to train its models by default. We do not use Gmail data to train, develop, or improve any AI or machine-learning model of our own.

What we store. Only the extracted ledger fields (date, vendor, amount, category, a short snippet, and the message ID used to prevent duplicates) are stored, in a private database that only the owner can read. We do not keep copies of full mailbox contents outside Gmail.

What we do not do. We do not sell Gmail data; we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties; we do not use it for advertising; and no person other than the owner reads the mailbox content, except where required to comply with law or to investigate abuse or a security issue.

Limited Use. Optimized Hub Media LLC's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access. The owner can revoke our access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Ledger data can be deleted on request or by the owner directly, and we delete requested data within 30 days.

5. Our Role as a Data Processor

With respect to the contact data of your CRM leads, Optimized Hub acts as a data processor on your behalf, not as the owner of that data. You remain the data controller responsible for the underlying legal basis to contact those individuals. We process this data solely to deliver the Service and do not use it for any other purpose. Messages sent through the Service may be drafted with the help of an AI model (Anthropic's Claude API), which receives only the limited lead details needed to write the message.

6. Third-Party Services We Use

To deliver the Service, we rely on the following categories of third-party providers, each governed by their own privacy and security practices:

  • Payment processing (Stripe)
  • Cloud hosting and database (Railway, Supabase)
  • Automation and workflow infrastructure (self-hosted)
  • Email (Google) and SMS delivery providers (Quo, Twilio)
  • AI processing (Anthropic)
  • Internal tracking and CRM tools (Notion, Google Sheets)

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

7. Data Retention

We retain client account information for as long as your subscription is active and for a reasonable period afterward for recordkeeping and legal compliance. Lead contact data processed on your behalf is retained only as needed to deliver the Service and is deleted or returned upon request following termination. Documents uploaded by borrowers for leads marked dead are deleted after 90 days. Clients are responsible for confirming that this retention meets their own regulatory obligations and for keeping their own backups.

8. Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect data we handle, including encrypted credential storage, row-level access controls on our databases, and compliance measures such as opt-in consent verification, Do-Not-Call scrubbing, and quiet-hours enforcement before any outbound message is sent.

9. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information at any time by contacting us. If you are a lead contacted through the Service and wish to opt out of further communication, you may reply STOP to any message or contact admin@optimizedhub.com.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for business use by licensed professionals and is not directed at individuals under 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to admin@optimizedhub.com.
