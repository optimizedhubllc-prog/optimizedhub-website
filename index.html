FAQ

What counts as a “conversation”?+

A real reply from one of your leads, like a question, a rate inquiry or “call me.” Opt-outs and one-word “no” replies don't count. You see every one on your dashboard.

How do you stay compliant?+

We keep consent records, scrub against Do-Not-Call lists, respect TCPA and Florida FTSA quiet hours, honor STOP instantly and include your NMLS disclosure in every message.

Which CRMs do you work with?+

Any CRM that can export a contact list (CSV). No CRM? The CRM add-on (+$100/mo) includes one, plus refi alerts for your past clients.

Can I cancel?+

Yes, anytime with 14 days' notice, in your Stripe customer portal or by email. Cancel before day 31 and you pay nothing. No long-term contract.

What happens after the free trial?+

Standard plan: billing starts on day 31. Founding Partners: if we've delivered at least 3 conversations within 30 days of your campaign launch, billing starts at $297/mo, locked for life; if not, your trial is extended until we do. Conditions apply.
